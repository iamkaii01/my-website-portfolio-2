04 / The process

Clear steps. Considered work.

  1. 01

    Align on the brief

    I study your product, offer, audience, script, references, existing footage, and deliverables before production begins.

  2. 02

    Plan the visuals

    I map the visual beats and define the product, character, wardrobe, environment, framing, and camera rules before generating.

  3. 03

    Produce & Edit

    I create and select the strongest visuals, then combine AI footage, real footage, B-roll, motion, captions, and direct-response editing.

  4. 04

    Review & deliver

    I review realism, product accuracy, continuity, pacing, script alignment, artifacts, captions, sound, and export quality before delivery.

05 / The details matter

Built with intention.

  • Realism Over AI Spectacle

    If the creative needs to feel native, I prioritize believable people, products, environments, gestures, and camera behavior over unnecessary polish.

  • Visuals Follow the Script

    Every shot should help communicate the problem, product, mechanism, proof, offer, or reason to keep watching.

  • Continuity Is Controlled

    Products, characters, wardrobe, framing, lighting, and environments are treated as production constraints, not left to chance.

  • Editing Finishes the Ad

    Generation creates assets. Editing controls pacing, structure, captions, sound, emphasis, and how the message lands.
